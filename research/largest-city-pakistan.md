# Largest city in Pakistan by population

**Answer: Karachi.**

Karachi, the capital of Sindh province, is Pakistan's largest city by population and
its main port and financial centre.

## Figures (2023 census)

| Rank | City | Population (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Karachi (Karachi Division) | ~20.4 million |
| 2 | Lahore (Lahore District) | ~13.0 million |

Karachi is ahead of the next-largest city, Lahore, by roughly 7 million people.

## Source

- Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, 7th Population and Housing Census (2023), the
  country's first digital census. Figures are rounded; city totals depend on whether
  the division/district or the city proper is counted, but Karachi ranks first either way.
